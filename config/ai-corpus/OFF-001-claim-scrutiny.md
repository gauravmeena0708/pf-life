Before recommending a claim, confirm that the member's KYC is verified, that the bank account is verified, that the amount is within the member's limit for the claim type, and that no freeze or open grievance affects the account.

Record the checks you made in the recommendation. The approving officer relies on this record, and a different officer must approve.

Return a claim for rework, with a reason, when information is missing; returning restarts the approval round.
