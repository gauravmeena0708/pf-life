A claim of up to ₹1,00,000 can be approved automatically when no advisory security check is open on the account. Larger claims go to your regional office.

At the regional office a dealing assistant reviews the claim first. Depending on the amount, a section supervisor or an accounts officer approves it; above ₹5,00,000 a second approver (an assistant PF commissioner or the officer in charge) also decides. The same officer never both recommends and approves.

After approval the cash section sends the payment to the bank account linked to your UAN. If the bank returns the payment, the cash section re-issues it; you can see every step in your claim timeline.

If a routine security check on recent account activity is open, even a small claim goes to an officer. This is not an accusation and does not change what you are entitled to.
