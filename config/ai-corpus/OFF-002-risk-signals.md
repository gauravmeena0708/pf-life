verified: checked against intelligence-service

An advisory risk signal means a rule noticed a pattern worth a human look, for example a new device followed by a contact change and a claim. It is not evidence and must not decide a claim.

A device shared by several members, such as a Common Service Centre kiosk, is normal and is shown only as context.

Review the claim on its merits. If something looks wrong, record it and refer the case; do not reject a claim only because a signal exists.
