Your employer files a monthly return (ECR) and pays a challan. Your passbook shows a contribution only after the payment is confirmed; until then the month appears as "filed by employer, awaiting payment".

Each entry shows the employee share and the employer share for the wage month, and a running balance. A claim that has been paid appears as a withdrawal.
