You can file a grievance from the Grievances page, optionally about a specific claim and with a supporting document. It goes to your regional office, whose public relations officer replies.

The illustrative service standard is 15 days at the regional office, 10 days at the zonal office and 7 days at head office. If the office has not helped, you can escalate the grievance to the next level. Head office is the top level.

After a grievance is resolved you can reopen it within 30 days if the problem remains.
