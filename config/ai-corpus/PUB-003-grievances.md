You can file a grievance from the Grievances page, optionally about a specific claim and with a supporting document. It goes to your regional office, whose public relations officer replies.

The illustrative service standard is {{grievances.sla_days.RO}} days at the regional office, {{grievances.sla_days.ZO}} days at the zonal office and {{grievances.sla_days.HO}} days at head office. If the office has not helped, you can escalate the grievance to the next level. Head office is the top level.

After a grievance is resolved you can reopen it within {{grievances.reopen_window_days}} days if the problem remains.
