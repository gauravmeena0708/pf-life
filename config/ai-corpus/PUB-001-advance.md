An advance for medical treatment (Form 31 in this demonstration) can be claimed while you are still employed. The amount you can ask for is limited to your own (employee) share of the balance, and never more than ₹10,00,000 in one claim.

Before you submit, the portal shows your maximum, the rule it used, and who will decide the claim. You then confirm the claim with a one-time code; the confirmation is tied to the exact amount, so a changed amount needs a new code.

These limits are illustrative values chosen for the demonstration. They are not the limits of the EPF Scheme, 1952.
