Three benefit contexts, one member’s life.

EPF

Provident fund

Employment-linked contributions, balances, advances, transfers and settlement. The central technology question is whether identity, entitlement and every financial movement remain traceable.

EPS

Pension

Service and entitlement records support pension benefits. Recurring pension, family pension and a withdrawal benefit are different journeys; they must not be represented as one generic claim.

EDLI

Insurance

A death-related insurance benefit context. A beneficiary may need PF, pension and insurance processes arising from the same life event, with separate records and decisions.

Orientation synthesis: docs/stakeholders.md, F04–F05 in docs/stakeholder-activities.yaml; source register PEN, EDLI, SCHEMES. No benefit rates or current eligibility are asserted here.

02 / Life events · 5 minutes

One person. Many records.
Several connected processes.

  1. 01

    Join employment

    The establishment and the worker are linked. Identity, member ID, UAN and KYC need consistency. Employer authority and worker identity are separate checks.

    F01 · F02
  2. 02

    Build the contribution history

    The employer prepares and approves a periodic ECR. Payment is confirmed, then the contribution ledger is posted. The member’s visible balance must reconcile to the underlying journal.

    F03
  3. 03

    Change jobs or correct a record

    Employment ends or moves. Exit details, transfers and profile corrections may involve several offices or institutions. The person remains the same while employment-specific records change.

    F02 · F04
  4. 04

    Request a benefit

    An advance, final settlement, pension or beneficiary claim needs applicable eligibility and evidence. Office scrutiny, authorised decision and financial execution are distinct responsibilities.

    F04 · F05
  5. 05

    Continue service, resolve exceptions

    Life certificates, bank changes, payment returns, grievances, late credits and family changes continue beyond the first decision. A terminal claim state is not the end of every member service.

    F05 · F08

Follow five questions through every lifecycle.

Who is the person and authorised actor? What business record is changing? Which authority permits the decision? What money moved or remains pending? Which evidence proves the outcome?

    Put boundaries around
    authority and records.

    ExperiencePublic / member / employer / office interfacesOne React web application contains multiple channels and role views.
    Access boundaryGateway + identity providerThe browser uses gateway sessions; the gateway brokers access to domain services.
    Business ownershipEmployer · Member · Contribution · Claim · Pension · ComplianceWorkflow, grievance, international, audit, reporting, platform and intelligence provide further business or shared capabilities.
    Supporting infrastructureService databases · events · object storage · simulationsThe demo uses service-specific database ownership on shared local infrastructure. Payment and external verification simulators are explicitly synthetic.

    Repository architecture: apps/web, apps/gateway, services/, packages/, contracts/, infra/. ADR 0001 describes the gateway boundary; ADR 0003 assigns the financial ledger to contribution-service; ADR 0006 describes generated contracts. These are repository decisions, not a discovered map of EPFO production systems.

    Record / responsibilityPOC ownerWhy the boundary matters
    Establishment and authorisationemployer-serviceKnow which person can prepare or attest for an employer.
    Member and employment recordmember-serviceKeep person identity distinct from employment and ledger accounts.
    Contribution and financial ledgercontribution-serviceBalanced integer-paise journals and reconciliation belong to one financial owner.
    Claim eligibility and decisionclaim-service + workflow-servicePreserve the decision basis, jurisdiction and approval round.
    Pension recordpension-serviceRecurring entitlement and payment history have a different lifecycle from a PF claim.

    Shared administrationplatform-servicePolicy and shared administration are distinct from workflow’s staff-posting record.

    External e-proceedings portal: proposed integration

    1. External portal owns notices, hearings, evidence and signed orders.
    2. An authenticated adapter accepts a versioned order and its evidence references.
    3. Compliance owns the corresponding business case / assessment mapping.
    4. A defined financial instruction reaches contribution-service for demand / ledger processing.
    5. Reconciled demand and payment status return to the originating portal.

    Current limitation: working compliance cases and the VISHWAS-specific DemandRaised.v1 path do not constitute a generic proceedings-order integration. Notice, hearing, order, appeal and recovery endpoints include planned contracts. A general, versioned assessment / order interface and production authentication, signature and replay controls need implementation and review.

    External HRM portal: proposed integration

    1. HRM provides a stable employee identifier and effective-dated posting / delegation changes.
    2. An adapter maps the employee to identity, office, roles and assignment revision.
    3. Workflow, the current POC posting owner, publishes an ordered, versioned staff-assignment change under a proposed revised event contract.
    4. Services revoke stale access and release or reassign affected cases.
    5. Entitlements and projections are reconciled, with acknowledgement and exceptions back to HRM.

    Current limitation: POST /api/v1/hrm/postings is an interactive, step-up-protected POC office operation for a known officer. Staff-posting events update service projections, but the reviewed implementation does not by itself update Keycloak role membership or active sessions, and needs assignment-revision / effective-date handling. It is not a ready external HRM feed.

    Teams can own domains independently while retaining shared contracts, security conventions, rule versioning and end-to-end assurance. Splitting physical repositories requires replacing root-level build and shared-package dependencies. An organisational ownership boundary can be introduced before that migration.

    Implementation review basis: gateway authentication handling; workflow-service/app/api/hr_routes.py; StaffPostingChanged consumers; compliance DemandRaised producer; contribution demand consumer. Integration sequences above are proposals, not recorded portal integrations.

    07 / The first week

    Questions that reveal
    the operating model.

    Day 1 · People & authority

    Who may decide?

    Ask an RO to walk one real anonymised case. Identify the initiating person, authorised checker, office jurisdiction, delegation version and escalation owner. Compare those answers with F01, F02 and F04.

    Day 2 · Money & reconciliation

    Where is the proof of payment?

    Trace one ECR from filing to bank response to member posting, and one claim from decision to debit to bank outcome. Ask what happens when a payment is confirmed but a posting fails.

    Day 3 · Exceptions & service

    What remains unresolved?

    Inspect returned claims, payment returns, freezes and reopened grievances. Establish whether dashboards distinguish these from rejection, successful settlement and actual resolution.

    Day 4 · Systems & access

    How does a transfer revoke access?

    Map HR identity to portal identity, effective posting, delegation and case ownership. Review machine integration audiences, signatures, replay protection, reconciliation and recovery.

    Day 5 · Evidence & roadmap

    What can the prototype prove?

    Review working, mock, planned and unknown endpoint inventory separately from passing tests. Assign owners to inferred activities, unrun case partitions, source validation and production integration gaps.

    These are suggested discovery activities. No real member data, credentials or production transaction is needed to use this handbook.
